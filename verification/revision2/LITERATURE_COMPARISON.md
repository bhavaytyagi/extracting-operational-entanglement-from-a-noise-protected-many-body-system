## Interpretation limits

The same abstract group may have different physical representations. Ref.Moharramipour2024 includes examples of non-Abelian finite groups; merely calling the present group dihedral does not establish a new theory or a priority distinction. The relevant distinction stated in the draft is its *spatial permutation action*, specified dissipative realization, product preparation and explicit conditional physical-pair decoder. Whether this combination is sufficiently new or important for PRL is an author/editor judgment requiring broader comparison; this bounded search does not decide it.

No claim that prior works lack every possible physical decoder is made. No unsupported claim of first observation, generic robustness or device advantage was introduced.
